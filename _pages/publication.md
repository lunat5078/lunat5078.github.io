**Emotion Is Not External Knowledge: Internal Attention Signals for Affective State Reasoning**  
<u>Wenqiu Tang</u>, Zhen Wan, Yihui Wang, Takahiro Komamizu, Ichiro Ide  
*AACL 2026 · Main Conference* · [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21347)
* Proposed training-free Self-targeted Internal Attention Boosting (SIAB) to causally localize and strengthen affective heads; improved prompt-matched baselines by up to +10.3 macro-F1 on ECC and GoEmotions.

**Facet-Level Persona Control by Trait-Activated Routing with Contrastive SAE**  
<u>Wenqiu Tang</u>, Zhen Wan, Takahiro Komamizu, Ichiro Ide  
*PAKDD 2026* · [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-92-1947-6_33) [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19157) [[Code]](https://github.com/lunat5078/BigFive-Personality-Facets-Dataset)
* Developed interpretable, facet-level persona control with contrastive sparse autoencoders and context-dependent routing; evaluated on a balanced, leakage-controlled 15,000-sample corpus.

**DeSRPA: Decoupled Speech Role-Playing Agent via Inference-Time Intervention**  
<u>Wenqiu Tang</u>, Zhen Wan, Takahiro Komamizu, Ichiro Ide  
*INTERSPEECH 2026* · [[Paper]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17669) [[Demo]](https://steeremo971-commits.github.io/emosteer-tts-demo/) [[Code]](https://github.com/steeremo971-commits/DeSRPA)
* Connected frozen Qwen3-4B and StyleTTS2 through separate cognitive and acoustic controls; evaluated controllable generation, speaker similarity, WER, latency, and human judgments.
